Privacy

Visitor information

This portfolio records visitors’ IP addresses, the page visited, and the time of the visit to understand site traffic.

What is recorded

When visitor logging is enabled, the portfolio records the IP address received by the hosting service, the page path, and the visit timestamp. It does not use this log to identify visitors by name.

Why and where it is stored

The information is used to review basic portfolio traffic and is stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database associated with this site. The database is accessible to the site owner through the Cloudflare account.

Retention

Records older than 30 days are deleted the next time the visitor log is used. Contact form messages are handled separately by the contact form provider.
